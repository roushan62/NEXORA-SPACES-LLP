Frequently Asked Questions

Design & Build means Nexora Spaces handles everything from start to finish. We create the designs (2D layouts, 3D visualizations), procure all materials, execute all civil work, carpentry, electrical, plumbing, and finishing. You get keys to a completely finished home — we don't just hand over drawings, we hand over a ready-to-move-in space.

1 BHK: 35-45 days
2 BHK: 45-60 days
3 BHK: 60-75 days
4+ BHK / Villa: 75-120 days
These are typical timelines from design approval to handover. Complex civil work or custom requirements may add time.

Our comprehensive warranty covers: all modular furniture (carpentry, hardware, hinges), electrical work (wiring, switches, lighting fixtures), plumbing (pipes, fittings), and finish defects. It doesn't cover misuse, natural wear of consumables, or external damage. This is over and above any manufacturer warranties on appliances.

We prefer to supply all materials as it ensures quality control, warranty coverage, and streamlined logistics. However, in certain cases (e.g., you want to use specific materials you already own), we can discuss exclusions. Any client-supplied materials fall outside our warranty coverage.
