Who We Are

Design That
Inspires Living

Nexora Spaces LLP was founded with a simple belief: everyone deserves a beautifully designed home. What started as a passion project in Bangalore has grown into one of India's most trusted residential interior fit-out companies.

We're not just interior designers — we're home architects who execute. Unlike agencies that hand over drawings, we deliver completed homes. Our team of 50+ designers, project managers, and craftsmen work together to transform spaces from concept to reality.

The name "Nexora" comes from the Latin "nexus" (connection) and "ora" (place) — a place where connections are made, where families gather, where memories are created.
