What every package covers

The eight spaces we detail in every home

A home is not finished when the living room photographs well. These are the spaces that decide whether it actually works day to day.

Living / hall

Seating layout, TV panelling, feature walls and the first impression from the door.

Modular kitchen

Work triangle, tall storage, counters, dado and appliance integration.

Bedrooms

Bedbacks, storage beds, wardrobes, side niches and reading light.

Puja room

A dedicated mandir — freestanding, niche or full room — detailed properly.

Washrooms

Vanities, mirrors, fittings, niches and anti-skid, easy-clean surfaces.

Walk-in wardrobe

Hanging, folding, drawers and sensor lighting planned around what you own.

Passage / corridor

The circulation that usually gets ignored — lit, finished and made useful.

The whole home

One palette, one lighting language, and joinery that lines up room to room.
