Local realities

What working in Delhi actually involves

The operational details that decide whether a project finishes on schedule here.

Older services need auditing

Aluminium wiring, 1990s GI plumbing and undersized DBs are the norm in South Delhi builder floors. We survey and quote for replacement rather than working around them.

Structural sensitivity

Many Delhi properties have load-bearing walls where clients assume partitions. We verify before any demolition is planned or priced.

Approval awareness

Facade changes, floor additions and certain structural work need municipal clearance. We flag anything in your brief that crosses that line.

Access constraints

Narrow lanes in GK, Lajpat Nagar and Rajouri restrict vehicle size and delivery timing. Material staging is planned around it.
