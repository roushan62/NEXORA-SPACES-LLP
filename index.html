How it works

Seven stages. No surprises in any of them.

Every project runs on the same documented system, whether it is a ₹6 lakh 1 BHK or a ₹2 crore villa.

Step 01

Consultation & site survey

A senior designer visits your home, measures every wall, and understands how your family actually lives. No sales script.

Day 1–3
Step 02

Concept & 3D design

Mood boards, layout options and photoreal 3D views of every room. You see the home before a single sheet is cut.

Day 4–10
Step 03

Itemised BOQ & contract

Line-by-line costing with brand names and specifications. Fixed price, signed scope, clear payment milestones.

Day 11–14
Step 04

Production in our factory

Modular units are CNC-cut, edge-banded and finished in a controlled environment — not assembled with dust on your floor.

Day 15–32
Step 05

Site execution

Civil, electrical, ceiling and painting run in a sequenced plan. Daily site supervision, weekly photo reports on WhatsApp.

Day 20–40
Step 06

Installation & styling

Modular units go in, lighting is commissioned, and our stylist dresses the home for your first walk-through.

Day 41–45
Step 07

Snag, handover & warranty

You raise a snag list, we close it, then you get warranty documents, care guides and a dedicated post-handover contact.

Our contractual standard is 45 days from design sign-off to handover for a 2–3 BHK, and 70–90 days for villas or full renovations. That clock starts after design freeze and the first payment milestone, not from the first phone call.

Because our modular woodwork is factory-made and arrives finished, site time is far shorter than carpentry-on-site. Delays are almost always caused by scope changes mid-project or society work-permission rules — both of which we flag upfront.
