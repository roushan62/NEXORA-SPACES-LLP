How it works

Seven stages, from first call to keys

You always know which stage you are in, who owns it, and what happens next.

Step 01

Consultation & site survey

A senior designer visits your home, measures every wall, and understands how your family actually lives. No sales script.

Day 1–3
Step 02

Concept & 3D design

Mood boards, layout options and photoreal 3D views of every room. You see the home before a single sheet is cut.

Day 4–10
Step 03

Itemised BOQ & contract

Line-by-line costing with brand names and specifications. Fixed price, signed scope, clear payment milestones.

Day 11–14
Step 04

Production in our factory

Modular units are CNC-cut, edge-banded and finished in a controlled environment — not assembled with dust on your floor.

Day 15–32
Step 05

Site execution

Civil, electrical, ceiling and painting run in a sequenced plan. Daily site supervision, weekly photo reports on WhatsApp.

Day 20–40
Step 06

Installation & styling

Modular units go in, lighting is commissioned, and our stylist dresses the home for your first walk-through.

Day 41–45
Step 07

Snag, handover & warranty

You raise a snag list, we close it, then you get warranty documents, care guides and a dedicated post-handover contact.

After a site survey we issue a written, itemised scope with every material, brand and finish named line by line. That document is what you approve, and it is what you pay against — the figure is specific to your home, so we do not publish rate cards that would only mislead you.

We are deliberately positioned as the value-driven option: budget-smart luxury, where the money goes into what you touch and see every day rather than into markup.

We commit to the fastest turnaround in the industry, and the specific handover date for your home is agreed at design sign-off and written into the contract. That clock starts after design freeze, not from the first phone call.

Because our modular woodwork is factory-made and arrives finished, site time is far shorter than carpentry-on-site. Delays are almost always caused by scope changes mid-project or society work-permission rules — both of which we flag upfront.

A documented, multi-year warranty on modular woodwork carcass and hardware — the ply, the edge banding, the hinges, the channels and the sliding systems — plus cover on site-executed services such as painting, false ceiling, electrical and plumbing.

It is transferable if you sell the property and it lives in your contract, not just in a brochure. Read the full warranty policy for exclusions like water damage or third-party modifications.

We handle all of it under one contract. That includes demolition, masonry, waterproofing, concealed plumbing and electrical, false ceiling, painting, flooring, modular joinery, puja room detailing and final styling. One team, one accountability line, one warranty.
